A French translation of the NES/Famicom adaptation of Final Fantasy VII.
Built on the existing Final Fantasy VII: Advent Children project.
The French adaptation draws on Néo-Midgar for dialogue and terminology.
Our work AI-assisted covers dialogues, menus, equipment, items, enemies and spell names.
French accents and additional glyphs were integrated into the game’s fonts.
Text pointers and storage were reorganized to accommodate the longer French text.
The resulting ROM fits within 2 MiB and retains mapper 163 compatibility.
Targeted engine fixes address bank switching, interrupts and reset behavior.
Validation combines automated checks with Mesen tests of gameplay, menus and saving.

Apply this patch to the English “Final Fantasy VII — Advent Children (Finished Version) v1.1” ROM, with its iNES header intact. It is not intended for direct application to the original Chinese NJ063 ROM
